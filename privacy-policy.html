Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Eagles Point Camp (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we handle when you visit this website or send us a booking enquiry, and the choices you have. We aim to comply with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya.

1. Information we collect

  • Booking enquiry details that you type into our forms: your name, email address, phone number, preferred accommodation, dates, number of guests and any special requests.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email.
  • Basic technical data that your browser sends to any website, such as your IP address and device type, which our hosting provider may log for security and reliability.

2. How the enquiry forms work

When you submit a form on this site, your details are placed into a pre-written WhatsApp message addressed to our booking number. The message is sent only when you choose to send it from WhatsApp. This website does not store your enquiry on its own server. WhatsApp is operated by Meta Platforms and is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and check availability and rates.
  • To confirm, manage and host your booking.
  • To keep records required by law and to resolve disputes.
  • To improve our service and this website.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Sharing your information

We share information only with people who need it to host you (for example staff and activity providers), with service providers who help us run the website, and with authorities where the law requires it. The website uses Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map, and your browser connects to Google when these load.

5. How long we keep it

We keep booking correspondence for as long as needed to serve you and meet our legal and accounting obligations, then delete or anonymise it.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict its processing, and withdraw consent. To do so, message us on WhatsApp at +254 719 215 135. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

8. Children

This website is intended for adults planning a stay. Please do not submit a child’s personal information through our forms.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows when it last changed.

10. Contact

Eagles Point Camp, Lake Elementaita, Nakuru County, Kenya. WhatsApp and phone: +254 719 215 135.

Getting here

Directions

Eagles Point Camp sits on a cliff above Lake Elementaita in the Great Rift Valley, in the Gilgil–Elementaita area of Nakuru County. It is about 126 km from Nairobi and about 20 km from Nakuru National Park.

Open the map for turn-by-turn directions from wherever you are, or message us on WhatsApp and we will guide you in.

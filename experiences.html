Things to do

Nine ways to spend a day here.

From the stillness of an early-morning birdwatch to the rush of abseiling off the cliff, Eagles Point has something for every pace.

Availability can vary with the season and the weather, so tell us what you have in mind when you book and we will help you plan.

Bird watching

Lake Elementaita is part of the Kenya Lake System, a UNESCO World Heritage Site recognised for its birdlife. Flamingos and pelicans gather on the shallow water below the cliff, and early mornings from the balcony are the best seat in the house.

Boating

Take to the water for a different view of the craters and the shoreline. Ask us which boating options are available during your stay.

Cycling

Quiet tracks and open Rift Valley country make for easy, scenic rides. Pedal out along the ridge and return for a late lunch on the balcony.

Quad biking

For a little adrenaline, head out on quad bikes across the dry, open ground around the camp. Closed shoes and a sense of adventure are all you need.

Wilderness treks

Walk the hills and craters around the lake, from a gentle morning stroll to a longer day on foot. The path from the camp down to the water is a favourite.

Rock climbing

The cliffs that hold the camp are also a playground. Climbing is offered for guests who want to test themselves against the Rift Valley rock.

Abseiling

Step off the edge, on a rope, with the lake spread out below you. Abseiling is one of the camp’s signature adventures.

Sports & fitness

Football, volleyball and basketball for groups, plus fitness training in the fresh Rift Valley air, which makes the camp a good fit for teams and school groups.

Camping & campfires

Pitch a tent above the lake and end the day around a fire under a very big sky. Camping is a true experience here, with the sunrise as your alarm clock.

Dining

Meals with the best view in the valley.

Our restaurant balcony looks out across Lake Elementaita, and the food is freshly prepared to match: simple, honest and generous. Vegetarian options are available.

Breakfast with the flamingos, a long lunch after a morning walk, supper as the sky turns gold.

For the best experience, tell us what you would like when you book. Planning ahead lets the kitchen prepare for dietary needs, celebrations and groups. Ask about packed lunches for days out walking or birdwatching.

Getting here

Directions

Eagles Point Camp sits on a cliff above Lake Elementaita in the Great Rift Valley, in the Gilgil–Elementaita area of Nakuru County. It is about 126 km from Nairobi and about 20 km from Nakuru National Park.

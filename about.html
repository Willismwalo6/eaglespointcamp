Our story

A camp on the edge of the Rift Valley, built to bring you closer to the lake, the birds and the sky.

Where it begins

A camp on the edge of the cliff.

Eagles Point Camp takes its name from the kind of place it is: a high, open vantage point from which the whole valley can be seen at once.

The camp stands above the cliffs of Lake Elementaita, in the Great Rift Valley of Nakuru County, with a panoramic view across the lake to the ancient craters that surround it. Below the cliff, a path leads down to the water, which glimmers like glass in the early light.

Everything here was shaped by that view. The cottages face it, the balconies are built for it, and the day is organised around it: first light, a slow breakfast, a walk to the shore, and an evening that ends under more stars than most of us ever see.

Where the cliff meets the lake, the day slows down.

The place

Lake Elementaita, a quiet soda lake.

Elementaita lies between Lake Naivasha and Lake Nakuru in the Rift Valley, and is the calm one of the three soda lakes.

Together with Nakuru and Bogoria, it forms the Kenya Lake System, a UNESCO World Heritage Site recognised for its birdlife. Flamingos and pelicans gather on its shallow water, and the shoreline changes colour with every hour of the day.

It is a landscape that rewards patience: sit on the balcony with a cup of coffee and a pair of binoculars, and the lake does the rest.

How we host

Built lightly, hosted warmly.

Five self-contained cottages, built from eco-friendly materials in a traditional setting, so the camp sits gently in its landscape.

Each cottage has a work desk, a seating area and a furnished balcony. Beyond the cottages there is room to camp, a restaurant looking out over the water, and space for football, volleyball and basketball.

The picnicker finds closeness with nature here. The camper finds an adventure, the student and researcher find a place to think, and the traveller finds a home away from home. That has always been the idea.

A day at Eagles Point

From first light to last embers.

Dawn

First light

Coffee on the balcony while the lake turns from silver to gold and the birds begin to stir.

Morning

Out and about

Walk the path to the water, cycle the ridge, or head out on a wilderness trek.

Golden hour

The long light

A late lunch or an early supper at the restaurant, with the whole valley glowing below.

Night

Fire and stars

Gather around the campfire and look up. There is very little between you and the sky.

Within reach

Easy to find, hard to leave.

The camp is about 126 km from Nairobi and roughly 20 km from Nakuru National Park, which makes it an easy weekend away or a restful base on a longer safari through the Rift Valley.

From here, Lake Nakuru, Lake Naivasha, Hell’s Gate and the Kariandusi prehistoric site are all within reach, and the lake itself is a short walk below your balcony.

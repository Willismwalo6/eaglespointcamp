Accommodation 01

Single Cottage Room

1 guestSingle bedLake-view balcony

Designed for the guest travelling alone, the Single Cottage Room is a calm, self-contained space in one of our eco-built cottages. A work desk sits by the window, a seating area invites you to slow down, and the furnished balcony opens onto the lake and the ancient craters beyond it.

Wake to the light over Lake Elementaita, walk the path down to the water before breakfast, and return to a room that is simple, warm and entirely yours.

Camping

Camp above the lake.

Prefer canvas? Camping at Eagles Point is an experience in its own right: a tent on the cliff top, the lake below, and a sky full of stars.

Campers are welcome on the grounds, with campfire evenings, the restaurant close by and every activity at the camp within walking distance. It is a favourite with groups, schools, researchers and families.

Getting here

Directions

Eagles Point Camp sits on a cliff above Lake Elementaita in the Great Rift Valley, in the Gilgil–Elementaita area of Nakuru County. It is about 126 km from Nairobi and about 20 km from Nakuru National Park.

Open the map for turn-by-turn directions from wherever you are, or message us on WhatsApp and we will guide you in.

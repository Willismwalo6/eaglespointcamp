About the camp

Nature’s Beauty, Reimagined.

Perched on a cliff above Lake Elementaita, Eagles Point Camp looks out over the glassy soda lake and the ancient craters that frame it. Our eco-built cottages, open balconies and unhurried days are made for people who want the Rift Valley at its quietest.

Come for the birdlife, the long walks and the adventure, or simply for the silence. However you spend your days, the view stays with you.

Dining

Meals with the best view in the valley.

Our restaurant balcony looks out across Lake Elementaita, and the food is freshly prepared to match: simple, honest and generous. Vegetarian options are available.

Breakfast with the flamingos, a long lunch after a morning walk, supper as the sky turns gold.

For the best experience, tell us what you would like when you book. Planning ahead lets the kitchen prepare for dietary needs, celebrations and groups. Ask about packed lunches for days out walking or birdwatching.
